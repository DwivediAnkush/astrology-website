Kundli Reading

Our Approach to Kundli Reading

The APP Formula combines three powerful dimensions to create a deeper and more practical understanding of your Kundli.

Astrology × Psychology × Philosophy

A — Astrology

Planetary placements, houses, yogas, dashas, transits, strengths and doshas reveal the structural patterns within your chart.

P — Psychology

Your chart is interpreted through behavioural patterns, emotional tendencies, decision-making and relationship dynamics — translating planetary patterns into real-life experiences.

P — Philosophy

Astrology is also about understanding purpose, choices, karma and perspective — helping you understand why certain patterns exist and how to respond to them consciously.

From Chart to Clarity

Your Kundli is first studied house by house, then individual placements are connected through yogas, dashas, timing and planetary influences.

The APP Formula turns these patterns into a practical understanding of your career, relationships, marriage, finances and life direction.
