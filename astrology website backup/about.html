About

Where Engineering Discipline Meets Ancient Practice

An engineer by training and a Vedic astrologer by practice — 4+ years of dedicated study and more than 50,000 charts studied along the way.

Vedic astrology has always been a system — a precise map of planetary positions translated into patterns that repeat across thousands of charts. That's what drew an engineer's mind to it in the first place: not the mysticism, but the method. Every birth chart is structured data, and reading one well means recognising patterns others might miss.

Over 4+ years of dedicated practice and more than 50,000 charts studied, that pattern recognition has been tested, applied and refined — across career questions, relationships, marriage compatibility, financial timing, and health-related guidance — using Vedic astrology, psychology, and philosophy.

APP formula used: Astrology + Psychology + Philosophy used to guide clients with clarity, practical thinking and emotional understanding.

A consultation here isn't performance. It's a structured read of your chart, explained plainly, followed by guidance you can actually act on.
