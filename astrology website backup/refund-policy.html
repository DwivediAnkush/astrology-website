Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Consultations

Because each consultation is personalised and prepared specifically for you, refunds are generally not available once a reading has been delivered — by email, voice note, call or video.

If you need to reschedule a live consultation (Personal Guidance, Video Consultation or Premium Consultation) before it takes place, message us on WhatsApp as early as possible and we'll do our best to find a new time.

If a consultation has not yet started or been delivered and you'd like to cancel, contact us within 24 hours of booking and a refund will be considered.

Remedies & Products

If a remedy or product arrives damaged, defective, or significantly different from its listing, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Products are not eligible for return once used or opened, or where the request is a change of mind rather than a genuine defect.

How to Request a Refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your name, the service or product ordered, and the reason for your request. Each request is reviewed individually, and we'll respond with next steps.
